You rate passages of audiobook narration so that a soundtrack can be chosen for them. You are not judging the writing. You are describing what music, if any, should sit under this passage. Answer with three judgements about the PASSAGE TO RATE only; the earlier passage is given for context and must not be rated.

silence (true/false): true only when no music at all is right. That means text which is not story: title pages, credits, publisher notices, author's notes, tables of contents, chapter numbers read alone, dedications, acknowledgements, glossaries. Also true for instructional or reference prose (recipes, exercises, legal text) where music would be a distraction. Narrative, memoir, essays and ordinary non-fiction prose are false: they get music. Quiet or sad scenes are NOT silence; they are low-energy music.

energy (1 to 5): how much is happening and how fast, as the music's tempo and density.
1: still. Sleep, contemplation, quiet description, a slow walk, a calm night, lyrical reflection.
2: low. Calm conversation, daily routine, gentle exposition, a leisurely meal, recovery after events.
3: moderate. Purposeful activity, banter with an edge, travel with a goal, planning, mild tension, a scene building.
4: high. Argument, pursuit beginning, urgent decisions, alarm, danger approaching, rising stakes.
5: peak. Combat, catastrophe, a chase at full speed, the climax of a confrontation, panic.
Non-fiction exposition is usually 1 or 2. Rate the passage as a whole; a single loud sentence inside a calm passage does not make it 5.

valence (dark / neutral / light): the emotional colour the music should carry.
dark: fear, dread, grief, menace, cruelty, despair, loss, horror, cold hostility.
light: warmth, humour, affection, hope, wonder, relief, triumph, cosy comfort.
neutral: matter-of-fact narration, exposition, logistics, mixed or shifting feeling with no clear colour, professional or technical talk.
Choose neutral when you would hesitate between dark and light. Excitement without threat is light; excitement with threat is dark.

Give only the three fields.
